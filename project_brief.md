\# Project Brief

\#\# Project Title

AI-Assisted Drug Interaction Awareness: A Pharmacist-Led Healthcare AI Foundation Project

\#\# 1\. Background

Medication safety requires consideration of both prescription and non-prescription medicines.

Patients may not always mention OTC products when presenting a prescription. They may also assume that a familiar medicine is appropriate because they have used it previously.

This project explores how Generative AI can support medication-safety education while emphasizing the importance of evidence verification, pharmacist review, and human oversight.

\---

\#\# 2\. Fictional Educational Scenario

A fictional 68-year-old patient presents a prescription containing \*\*escitalopram\*\*, an SSRI antidepressant prescribed by her physician.

During a dry-cough episode, she verbally asks the pharmacist for an OTC cough syrup containing \*\*dextromethorphan\*\*, which she has used previously for similar symptoms.

The patient considers the cough syrup familiar and routine. However, the pharmacist recognizes the need to review the patient's current medication profile and evaluate potential interaction concerns before making a professional recommendation.

\---

\#\# 3\. Medication-Safety Focus

The project examines the potential interaction concern associated with:

\*\*Escitalopram \+ Dextromethorphan\*\*

The purpose is not to determine an individual patient's clinical outcome.

Instead, the project demonstrates the importance of:

\* Reviewing the complete medication profile  
\* Considering prescription and OTC medicines together  
\* Consulting reliable drug-information resources  
\* Recognizing uncertainty and patient-specific factors  
\* Applying pharmacist judgment and human oversight

\---

\#\# 4\. Project Goals

\#\#\# Goal 1 — Prompt Engineering

Compare a weak prompt with an engineered prompt and examine how additional context and safety requirements influence the AI response.

\#\#\# Goal 2 — AI Output Evaluation

Evaluate real AI-generated responses for:

\* Relevance  
\* Completeness  
\* Structure  
\* Clinical context  
\* Safety awareness  
\* Appropriate uncertainty  
\* Professional usefulness

\#\#\# Goal 3 — Pharmacist Oversight

Demonstrate why AI output must be reviewed and verified before it is used in a healthcare context.

\---

\#\# 5\. Project Workflow

1\. Create a weak prompt with limited context.  
2\. Record the real AI-generated response.  
3\. Identify limitations in the response.  
4\. Design an engineered prompt with clear context and safety boundaries.  
5\. Record the real AI-generated response.  
6\. Compare the two outputs.  
7\. Evaluate both outputs using professional judgment.  
8\. Verify important information using reliable medical sources.

\---

\#\# 6\. Role of AI

AI may support:

\* Educational content drafting  
\* Information organization  
\* Structured question generation  
\* Identification of topics requiring further review

AI should not function as an independent clinical decision-maker.

\---

\#\# 7\. Role of the Pharmacist

The pharmacist remains responsible for:

\* Reviewing prescription and OTC medicines  
\* Identifying possible interaction concerns  
\* Assessing the complete medication profile  
\* Consulting current and authoritative resources  
\* Considering patient-specific factors  
\* Interpreting the clinical relevance of available evidence  
\* Providing an appropriate professional medication-safety assessment

\---

\#\# 8\. Project Limitations

This project:

\* Uses one fictional educational scenario  
\* Evaluates AI-generated information rather than a validated clinical system  
\* Does not determine individual patient risk  
\* Does not provide patient-specific treatment recommendations  
\* Does not replace professional healthcare assessment

\---

\#\# 9\. Final Learning Outcome

This project demonstrates that Prompt Engineering can improve the quality and structure of AI-generated information. However, improved output is not equivalent to verified clinical information.

\> Responsible healthcare AI requires evidence verification, human oversight, and professional judgment.
